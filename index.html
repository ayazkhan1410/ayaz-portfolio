// ABOUT

  Developer profile
  
Background & approach

  

    

      
I'm Ayaz Khan, a backend developer from Bahawalpur, Pakistan. I studied Information Technology at The Islamia University of Bahawalpur and started my career on a travel booking platform, building hotel, flight and auth APIs.

      
Most of my work since then has been on systems that talk to other systems: EDI integrations with large US retailers, healthcare claim files sent over SFTP, and Kafka streams feeding AI services. That work taught me to care about queues, retries, slow queries and clear logs.

      
I like backend code that is simple to read, easy to test, and keeps running when traffic or partners misbehave.
